About Clearelay

Communication built around real people.

Clearelay is a human-driven communications and scheduling platform built to make work simpler for small businesses. We bring customer conversations, team communication, follow-ups, and scheduling into one convenient place so business owners and their teams can spend less time managing disconnected tools and more time helping customers and growing their business.

People are at the center of what we do. Our platform helps businesses stay organized while keeping every customer interaction personal, because the best communication still happens when people talk with people.
