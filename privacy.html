Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clearelay (“Clearelay,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website, products, and services (collectively, the “Services”).

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, company information, account credentials, and messages or other content you submit through the Services. We may also collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, and usage information.

How we use information

We use information to provide, operate, secure, improve, and support the Services; communicate with you; respond to requests; prevent fraud and misuse; and comply with applicable law.

Text messaging and mobile information

If you choose to opt in to receive text messages from Clearelay, we use your mobile number and consent information to send the messages you requested and to administer the messaging program.

We do not sell or share your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in data, or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their promotional or marketing purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties except service providers that help us deliver the requested messaging service, such as messaging platforms and telecommunications carriers, and only as needed to provide that service or comply with law.

How we share information

We may share information with vendors that perform services for us (such as hosting, analytics, customer support, and message delivery), when required by law or a valid legal process, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transaction. These recipients may use information only for the purposes described above or as required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to meet legal, accounting, or reporting obligations. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may opt out of promotional emails by following the unsubscribe instructions in the email. To stop text messages, reply STOP to a message from us. For help with a text messaging program, reply HELP or contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date.

Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices can be sent to support@clearelay.com.
